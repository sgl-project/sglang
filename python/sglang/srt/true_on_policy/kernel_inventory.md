## Family program modules

- `sglang.srt.true_on_policy.attention`: Shared dense TOP attention forward for packed training and paged serving.
- `sglang.srt.true_on_policy.dsv4`: Server-start checks and MoE bindings for DeepSeek-V4-Flash's TOP forward.
- `sglang.srt.true_on_policy.dsv41`: Server-start checks for DeepSeek-V4.1-Flash's TOP forward.
- `sglang.srt.true_on_policy.fp8`: Block-FP8 forward for true-on-policy serving, shared with the trainer.
- `sglang.srt.true_on_policy.gdn_chunked`: TOP GDN: rollout decode follows FLA's fixed-tile chunk-64 prefill bit for bit.
- `sglang.srt.true_on_policy.gdn_contract`: Admission for the Qwen GDN TOP program; the shared no-split rule (``contracts``) owns the engine layout.
- `sglang.srt.true_on_policy.glm`: Server-start checks for GLM's TOP forward (BF16 or block-FP8 checkpoints).
- `sglang.srt.true_on_policy.glm5_next`: Admission of GLM-5.3-Flash (``glm5_next``) under TOP: what its mHC, MoE, checkpoint and MTP programs require.
- `sglang.srt.true_on_policy.glm5_next_dsa`: GLM-5.3-Flash (glm5_next) DSA under TOP: one indexer, selection and sparse-attention program for prefill, decode and the trainer.
- `sglang.srt.true_on_policy.glm_moe`: TOP bindings for the GLM (DeepSeek-V2 family) gate, MoE block and model admission.
- `sglang.srt.true_on_policy.kda_chunked`: TOP KDA (GLM-5.3-Flash): rollout decode follows the pinned KDA chunk-64 prefill bit for bit.
- `sglang.srt.true_on_policy.moe_ep`: Shared TOP EP operation constraints and qualified expert configurations.
- `sglang.srt.true_on_policy.ple`: Stands in for the stock PLE fusions (kernels/ops/elementwise/qwen4_gate.py, mamba/qwen4_short_conv.py): they redo the eager BF16 chain (BF16 norms, products, gate and gated value, sign(0) = 0) and run only in decode or verify, while prefill stays eager, so prefill and decode would differ.
- `sglang.srt.true_on_policy.qsa`: Pinned Qwen Sparse Attention (QSA) program shared by rollout and trainer.
- `sglang.srt.true_on_policy.qwen_moe`: TOP bindings for the Qwen3 MoE sparse block: admission, the shared EP1 forward and DeepEP routing.
- `sglang.srt.true_on_policy.vision`: The frozen vision tower's TOP program: serving's vision layers and the trainer's tower call these functions.
