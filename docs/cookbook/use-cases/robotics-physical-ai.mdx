## Action policies

An action policy runs inside the control loop. It takes camera frames, the robot state, and a language instruction, and returns a chunk of future actions for the robot to execute. All of the policies below are served through the [action API](/docs/sglang-diffusion/api/openai_api#action-generation-sglang-extension).

## World models and simulation

A world model predicts how a scene evolves, as video, from a starting observation and control inputs such as camera motion or robot actions. It runs outside the control loop, for example to roll out a scene under a given sequence of controls.
